Transparency

How the ATS compatibility score is calculated

The analyser is deterministic. It inspects the resume's content and structure, awards points per check and reports every deduction. The same resume always produces the same score, and no score is invented, randomised or padded. It is an estimated resume-quality and ATS-compatibility measure — not output from an employer's proprietary ATS.

CategoryWhat earns pointsMax
ContactName, valid email, phone, location, one professional link12
Career objectivePresent, 20–85 words, names a role or relevant skills10
EducationEntries, institution + degree, years, CGPA/percentage15
SkillsFive+ technical skills, eight+ total, soft skills, role relevance15
ProjectsPresence, depth, described work, technologies, link15
CertificationsAny certification, issuer and date5
ExperienceEntries, responsibilities, measurable achievements8
KeywordsMatch against the target role's required skills10
FormattingSelectable text, bullets, line length, no tables, contact in text, dates6
ReadabilityBullet length, action verbs, concise objective, professional voice4

Score bands

  • 85–100 Excellent — ATS ready
  • 70–84 Strong — small fixes needed
  • 55–69 Moderate — several gaps
  • Below 55 Needs work

Notes on fairness

  • Freshers cannot earn the 8 experience points; that is explained in the deduction rather than hidden.
  • Keywords are matched with synonyms and whole-term comparison, never by counting stuffing.
  • A scanned PDF without selectable text cannot be read, and the score says so instead of guessing.
  • Changing a template does not change the score; changing real content does.
