About CareerGuide

Clarity about your next step — without the guesswork

CareerGuide is the career-guidance module of the AI Resume Builder and Career Recommendation System. It helps students understand their strengths, identify suitable career paths, discover missing skills, improve their knowledge and plan their professional future.

  • Understand your strengths — the skills you already have are shown against every career.
  • Identify suitable paths — five ranked matches, each with a clear reason.
  • Discover missing skills — the exact gaps for the roles you want, not vague advice.
  • Improve your knowledge — staged topics, projects and official learning links.
  • Plan your future — a roadmap that starts today and ends with an interview-ready portfolio.

Built as an academic project

Developed by two B.Tech students in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science. The recommendation engine is intentionally explainable: a fixed weighted formula over a curated 40-career dataset, with no random or manufactured percentages.

Results are guidance, not a verdict. Use them to explore, then verify requirements with real job descriptions and people working in the field.
